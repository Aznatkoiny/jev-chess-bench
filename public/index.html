How to read the rating

The rating is provisional and relative to this benchmark's opponent pool. The experiment configuration records the initial rating and update rule. The displayed interval reflects available sample uncertainty; it does not establish an absolute playing strength.

Synthetic harness runs validate the app and are not evidence of Jev's playing performance. Smoke runs check the live integration; tournaments are the planned comparison.
